# Additional vintage charts for the current inventory

Based on the newly supplied Flickinger export, the largest missing coverage is:

- **Burgundy Côte de Beaune reds**: Pommard, Volnay, Beaune, Corton and related appellations. The provided red Burgundy chart is specifically Côte de Nuits and cannot stand in for these wines. Around 700 red Burgundy offers remain unassessed.
- **Other Tuscan reds**: Bolgheri / Super Tuscans / coastal Tuscany, with the chart's actual geographic and wine-style scope. The supplied Tuscany chart is Chianti/Chianti Classico, not every Tuscan wine. Current export rows often identify only Italy, so wine-level region labels or a region mapping table will also be useful.
- **Piedmont Barolo/Barbaresco: 2022 and 2023**. The supplied chart ends in 2021; the inventory has newer wines.
- **Champagne: 2020 and 2021**, where a published rating exists. The supplied chart ends in 2019. NV/MV wines do not receive a year rating.
- Optional: **Veneto/Amarone and Sicily/Etna**, with red/white scope stated; and older Bordeaux/Rhône/Piedmont years preceding the supplied chart ranges.

PDFs, screenshots or a spreadsheet are all usable. Keep the publication name, region/subregion, red/white or grape scope, year, original rating and any provisional or drinking-status symbols visible. An updated chart replaces its own observations while retaining history. No scores will be invented for missing years.

California requires a confirmed variety/style: the existing Cabernet/Franc, Pinot Noir and Chardonnay charts are already saved. Generic proprietary blends and white wines of another grape cannot automatically use those charts. Confirming varieties for those individual wines will help more than another copy of the same chart.
