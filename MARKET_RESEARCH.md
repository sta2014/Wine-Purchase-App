# U.S. public-web market research

The pricing route now enqueues public-retailer research instead of requiring a commercial price feed. Wine-Searcher is not a pricing dependency. Optional Brave search remains disabled unless WINE_ENABLE_PAID_SEARCH=true is explicitly configured; its results remain unverified leads. Existing manual offers, critic scores, vintage charts, journal records and Flickinger imports are preserved.

## Coverage and live verification

The registry contains 50 distinct curated candidates, not a claim about market share or completed price coverage. MacArthur/Bassin's is one business. Wine-Searcher, Sherry-Lehmann and Cult Wines were replaced with Belmont, D&M, Le Du's and Verve because the candidate list also contained a duplicate. Forty-five candidates were blocked by cloud egress before website verification, and remain inactive. Their domains are saved as additive environment-network requirements. Network access is not collection permission.

Three retailer-specific parsing profiles exist (JJ Buckley, K&L, Benchmark), sharing a schema.org Product/Offer parser and configurable, bounded discovery strategies. **Only JJ Buckley has a successfully live-validated product adapter and is enabled.** Its public policy and robots were reviewed; no automated-retrieval prohibition was found in the accessible policy. The connector limits personal research to public product pages and respects subsequent blocks. K&L terms redirect to shop.klwines.com and its native search route is robots-restricted; activation remains pending. Benchmark discovery was reachable, but the sampled product URL redirected and provided no product offers; activation remains pending. Flickinger is excluded from its own external benchmark.

Actual small-scale direct checks: JJ Buckley 2019 Conn Creek Special Selection showed $149.94 and PreOrder (excluded from current benchmark). JJ Buckley 2020 Belair-Monange Saint-Emilion showed $234.94, USD, 750ML and explicit schema.org InStock; this was verified through the production adapter. These checks are retrieval examples, not claims about every imported wine or durable future availability. No external dated historical dataset was recovered. Historical prices are never guessed from page age.

## Operation

Use the running Node 24.5+ engine (`npm run engine`) and the app it serves or the proxied development app. Market research shows source access, verification, schedule, queue, progress and failures. Check access re-audits a source; Configure source requires documented terms review before activation. Robots is rechecked daily. Public requests never follow off-origin redirects or bypass access controls. Existing source URLs must be configured to their verified final endpoints.

GitHub Pages is static and cannot operate an unattended queue or fetch most retailers across browser CORS restrictions. In browser/manual mode the registry, manual verified offers, filters and history work; automatic controls clearly require a connected running engine. No always-on remote backend has been provisioned. A local desktop engine or existing Docker deployment costs no pricing API subscription. The browser research links remain available without credentials.

Schedules: twice daily, daily or weekly; disabled by default. Default cycle: 20 distinct beverage/volume/package identities, up to five enabled sources each, two concurrent jobs, five-second minimum per-origin request interval plus robots crawl-delay, five product pages per wine/source. Priorities consider purchasing rank, critics, vintage quality and missing/stale comparables. Normal searches use a 12-hour persistent result cache. Discovery sitemaps use a seven-day in-process cache. Retries use exponential backoff with at most three attempts; denied/robots-restricted jobs stop. A canceled in-flight request may finish within its timeout, but its observations are not committed. Restart recovers running jobs as queued. The scheduler runs only while the engine runs.

The HTTPS request layer uses normal TLS verification. In this cloud environment local DNS cannot resolve certain otherwise permitted merchant hosts. For only the fixed retailer-registry domains, a DNS-failure fallback uses the platform-supported HTTPS proxy through curl, without redirect following, secrets, authentication or disabled certificate verification. curl is already installed here and ships with current Windows; other local hosts must install it if that fallback is needed.

## Matching and valuation

Exact vintage and physical bottle volume are mandatory. Unknown format is excluded. Loose package counts may be normalized per bottle; OWC/package-value differences stay excluded. Full-designation fallback supports imported wines missing a producer field only when the complete normalized title, vintage, format and geographic/identity constraints agree. Strong fuzzy candidates require inspection and an explicit user decision; the research collector never auto-approves them. Les Forts de Latour remains different from Château Latour.

Only verified InStock or explicitly LimitedAvailability retail offers contribute. Likely stock, preorder, futures, sold, out-of-stock, historical and auctions remain separate. Currency, tax basis, stock timestamp and merchant provenance are mandatory. Deduplication keeps one lowest comparable offer per merchant. Median is primary; lowest, range, average, dispersion, and 10%-trimmed mean with at least five merchants are disclosed. Package savings multiply the per-bottle difference by actual bottle count. Outliers beyond max(3 scaled MAD, 50% of median) are flagged and retained, reducing confidence through dispersion; they can be excluded after inspection.

Default minimum comparables: three for moderate confidence, five for high confidence, also requiring numerical confidence >=0.7. These counts are configurable in Preferences. Thin samples cannot imply high confidence. Missing market/critic/vintage data contributes a disclosed neutral 50 with zero evidence coverage. Market discount is weighted once and multiplied by confidence. Quality and vintage remain independent weighted components.

Historical entries require original_observation_at and provenance to establish an external date. Undated old pages do not create history points. Local repeated observations retain prior prices and stock state. 30/90/180/365-day changes pair observations of the same offer, with a current reading within 48 hours and a real reading near/before the interval boundary; otherwise they display insufficient data. Auction channels are excluded from the retail history chart. Data can be exported in the engine backup.

## Persistence and migration

Existing SQLite data is preserved. Additive CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS migrations add retailer_registry, search_jobs, retrieval_failures and historical_market_prices, with due-job and wine/date indices. Immutable historical rows use INSERT OR IGNORE. Source configuration and queue also travel in the state/backup metadata. Restoring a backup disables source permissions and scheduling pending review. Price observations and valuation snapshots remain auditable. Research imports capture valuation history for the target wine only rather than rescoring every inventory listing for every job.

Important remaining limits: 45 source domains require applied network settings and fresh review; unverified native search paths are candidates, not validated endpoints; there is no configured external auction-history collector or recovered historical corpus; generic schema-only sources remain unactivated until a real product/stock/format test passes. The source adapters collect evidence only and do not guarantee every inventory wine has comparables.
