FREE WINE PURCHASING INTELLIGENCE — WINDOWS

1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted Wine Purchasing Intelligence folder.
3. Double-click Start Wine.exe. The first launch downloads a free official Node.js runtime and checks its published checksum. No installation commands, subscription or cloud account are needed.
4. The wine app opens in your browser. Upload your Flickinger Excel file. For a full inventory export, choose Complete inventory snapshot so offers missing from the new export stop appearing as available; their history remains.
5. Click Vintage charts, then Load Wine Spectator charts. Saved assessments persist across later imports.
6. Open Market research to see price-search progress and source errors. Current verified offers update rankings as searches finish. This is background research: thousands of wines take time, and some will have no verified comparable.

Keep the starter window open while researching prices. Closing it stops background searches; your data remains saved. Next time, double-click Start Wine.exe in the SAME extracted folder.

No hosting or pricing API is paid for or required. Searches run on your computer using permitted public retailer pages. Only JJ Buckley currently has a live-validated automatic adapter; other source candidates remain inactive pending access/format/stock validation. A missing price does not establish that there is no buying opportunity. Discounts compare recorded exact-vintage/bottle-format prices; taxes, shipping and sample size still matter.

Your inventory, chart ratings, price history and jobs are stored in data/wine-engine.sqlite in this folder. Keep that folder when updating the app. Export engine backups from the app. The GitHub Pages website has separate browser storage; its backup can be restored here if you want your previous manual reviews or corrections.

The launcher is built from desktop/launcher/main.go in the application repository. Its embedded official Node.js release archive checksum is verified before execution. Windows 10/11, 64-bit Intel/AMD, and an internet connection are required.
