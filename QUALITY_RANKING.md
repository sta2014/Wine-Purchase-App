# Three-factor quality policy

The only top-level weights are professional quality 60%, vintage quality 25%, and professional consensus/confidence 15%. All remain adjustable in the existing preferences form. Algorithm version 6 records the applied weights and provenance in quality history. No price, region preference, drinking-window, community or market-value term influences quality or tie breaking.

Professional quality uses score / scale × 100. Registered publications have equal weight; an unregistered professional publication has half credibility. Each publication contributes its median selected score, rather than giving prolific reviewers more weight. Verified reviews take precedence over relayed scores; within a provenance tier, explicitly final bottle reviews take precedence over preliminary reviews. Ranges use their lower end; plus notation provides no invented bonus. Individual observations remain inspectable. The Wine Independent is registered alongside existing critics. Consumer ratings never enter professional quality.

Vintage quality uses the existing normalized 0–100 regional assessment. Matching requires compatible country, geographic hierarchy, type/style and exact year. The most specific eligible geographic tier wins. Source originals, chart scope, fallback level and disagreements remain visible. A Côte de Nuits red chart cannot supply a Côte de Beaune red score; a Chianti chart cannot supply a generic Super Tuscan score. NV/MV is not applicable, rather than a missing year to guess.

Consensus = 100 × min(independent publications / 5, 1) × max(0, 1 − publication score spread / 15) × mean evidence reliability. Reliability includes identity matching, provenance (verified 1, relayed .6) and stage (final 1, unspecified .85, barrel/range .65). This measures evidence strength; average score level is excluded, so high agreement on poor scores cannot duplicate quality. Multiple providers repeating one publication do not create independent critics.

Missing components remain null. For a selected weight total of 100, available weight A is the sum of the weights whose scores are present. Overall quality = sum(weight × present score) / A, or unranked if A = 0. Effective weights are shown in Explain. Confidence is separately the sum of selected weight × component evidence confidence / 100, so missing evidence reduces confidence. Wines with professional quality and either two independent publications or a regional vintage assessment have sufficient evidence; others are provisional and follow sufficiently evidenced wines before score sorting. This prevents a single exceptional unsupported review from automatically ranking first. A complete verified filter additionally requires independent critic verification. Asking-price filters affect eligibility, never the score.

Legacy five-factor weights migrate to 60/25/15. A saved intermediate four-factor configuration moves its community weight into the critic weight, retaining vintage and consensus choices. Browser IndexedDB writes a recoverable pre-quality snapshot before committing migration; failures protect the original. SQLite takes a file snapshot and transactional metadata archive before removing dedicated pricing tables. No production migration has been performed. Old algorithm histories remain in the recovery archive rather than being shown as current quality observations.

Corrections preserve wine IDs: parenthesized, explicitly labelled release/reconditioning years do not masquerade as vintages. Real conflicting vintage years remain excluded. Reimport refreshes validated inventory metadata and exact retailer-review identity warnings. Geography masks only specifically recognized producer-name collisions and qualified lieu-dits, preserves true geographic conflicts, and extends the hierarchy for documented appellation names. These rules do not manufacture scores.

Source access: the approved JSON/feed layer retains robots, HTTPS, credential-host binding, TTLs and rate-limit handling. No live critic API is configured. A Wine Enthusiast policy request encountered an access challenge; it was not bypassed. Accessible James Suckling robots does not establish licensed access or API permission. Publication search links are manual leads; snippets never verify scores. Bulk reference imports are the practical expansion path until a genuinely approved data source is available.
